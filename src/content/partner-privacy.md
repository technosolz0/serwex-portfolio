# Serwex Partner Privacy Policy

_Last Updated: 20 August 2026_

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Serwex Partner**. We value the trust you place in us as a service provider, vendor, or technician on our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how **Serwex Partner** collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal and professional information when you use our mobile application (**Serwex Partner**) and partner portal.

By registering as a partner or using the **Serwex Partner** app, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

To connect you with customer service requests, manage your account, verify your identity, and process payouts, we collect the following categories of information:

### A. Personal & Identity Information
- **Contact Details**: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, emergency contact, and permanent/current address.
- **Government Verification Documents**: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID, Driving License, Passport, or other official identity and address proofs required for background verification and legal compliance.
- **Profile Data**: Profile picture, gender, language preferences, and professional experience.

### B. Professional & Service Details
- Service categories offered (e.g., electrical work, plumbing, cleaning, appliance repair).
- Work availability hours, coverage areas, service history, completed bookings, ratings, and customer reviews.

### C. Financial & Payout Information
- Bank account details (Account number, IFSC code, bank name, cancelled cheque image).
- UPI ID, PAN details for tax compliance (TDS deduction where applicable), payment histories, and platform commission records.

### D. Precise Location Data (Foreground & Background)
- **Background & Foreground Location Access**: The **Serwex Partner** app collects precise device location data even when the app is running in the background or closed.
- **Why We Need Location Data**:
  - To match you with customer service bookings in your immediate geographical vicinity.
  - To enable live tracking for customers so they can view your estimated time of arrival (ETA) while you are en route to a booking.
  - To verify job check-ins and check-outs at customer service locations.
  - To calculate accurate travel distance and optimize job routing.

### E. Device & System Data
- IP address, device hardware model, operating system version, app version, unique device identifiers, and push notification tokens.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

- **Service Matching & Dispatch**: Connecting your profile with nearby customers requesting services.
- **Identity & Background Verification**: Conducting KYC, identity validation, and background safety checks before account activation.
- **Live Tracking & Status Updates**: Sharing real-time location and job status with customers during active service assignments.
- **Payment Processing**: Calculating earnings, deducting platform commission, and transferring weekly/daily payouts directly to your bank account.
- **Communication & Support**: Sending job alerts, system updates, safety advisories, and responding to your support tickets.
- **Platform Integrity & Fraud Prevention**: Monitoring for fraudulent activities, unauthorized account access, or policy violations.
- **Legal Compliance**: Complying with tax reporting, government audits, and legal mandates.

---

## 4. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following scenarios:

- **With Customers**: Once you accept a booking, the customer will see your name, profile photo, contact number, customer ratings, and real-time location while you are traveling to the job site.
- **With Payment Partners**: Banking and payment gateway partners receive your bank account details solely to process direct deposit payouts.
- **With Verification Vendors**: Background check and identity verification service providers process your government IDs securely.
- **With Legal Authorities**: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the public.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement robust technical, physical, and administrative security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:

- **Encryption**: Sensitive data (such as bank details and government ID numbers) is encrypted during transmission and storage using industry-standard protocols.
- **Access Control**: Internal access to partner personal data is restricted strictly to authorized employees and systems on a need-to-know basis.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

- We retain your personal data for as long as your **Serwex Partner** account remains active or as needed to provide you with services.
- If you wish to deactivate or permanently delete your partner account, you can submit a deletion request via the app settings or by emailing **support@thelifetrackr.com** or **serwex.in@gmail.com**.
- Note that certain transaction and tax records may be retained for statutory periods as mandated by applicable laws.

---

## 7. Your Rights & Choices

- **Access & Correction**: You can view and update your personal details and bank info directly in the **Serwex Partner** app.
- **Location Permissions**: You can disable location permissions in your device settings; however, disabling background location will prevent you from receiving new service requests or accepting bookings.
- **Push Notifications**: You can manage notification preferences for job alerts and marketing updates in the app settings.
